I am an aspiring innovation economist with research interests in artificial intelligence, technological change, occupational regulation, and the institutions that shape innovation and economic growth. My work explores how emerging technologies influence labor markets and public policy, with a particular interest in the intersection of AI, regulation, and innovation.

I'm a Legislative Analyst and Predoctoral Scholar at the [Knee Regulatory Research Center](https://knee.wvu.edu/). I collect and analyze regulatory data for occupations included in the Center's occupational licensing database. I also contribute to policy briefs, op-eds, and other editorial publications supporting the Center's research agenda. As a Predoctoral Scholar, I collaborate with colleagues on academic research projects, primarily focused on the intersection of artificial intelligence and occupational regulation.
